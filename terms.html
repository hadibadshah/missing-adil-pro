🛡️ 1. Replacement Warranty Policy

Every product purchased on missingadil.store includes a 100% full-term replacement warranty matching the exact duration selected during checkout (1 Month, 3 Months, or 1 Year).

  • If your assigned account credentials lose premium access or experience technical access issues, you are entitled to a free replacement.

  • Modifying primary account security credentials (such as changing master passwords, recovery emails, or billing info) invalidates the replacement warranty.

👤 2. Shared Account Rules & Ethics

To maintain optimal performance and uptime for all users on shared accounts:

  • Users must strictly log in on one device at a time unless a multi-device plan was explicitly purchased.
  • Reselling, sharing assigned login credentials publicly, or attempting to lock out other screen users is strictly prohibited.
  • Violation of shared account safety guidelines will result in account suspension without replacement.

💵 3. Refund & Order Cancellation Policy

Due to the instant digital nature of subscription access codes and account credentials:

  • Once access credentials or activation invite links are delivered, orders are non-refundable.
  • If an account encounters a service disruption that cannot be resolved within 48 hours, a store credit or replacement for an equivalent digital tool will be issued.
